History

Founded in 2018 in Gqeberha (formaly Port Elizabeth), Eastern cape , Jahs Adoption centre was established to respond to the growing number of abandoned, vunerable and orphaned children across the Nelson Mandlea Bay region Including Kariega and Dispatch. The first centre for housing of the children in Korsten. The Centre moved to a new closed lot in the walmer area as a large warehouse where children eat, sleep and learn.

Mission Statment

How Jah Started, Jah Started as an adoption centre in founded in 2018. we started when we noticed the high amount of children living on the street. we decided it was time for a change, thats why we launched a small warehouse in Korsten before moving to Wlamer, where our new warehouse is built.

Vision Statment

A caring orphanage where children are grown up in a safe place and cared for in a learning enviroment and assisted in the hopes of finding a new home for these children one day.
